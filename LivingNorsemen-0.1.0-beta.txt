LivingNorsemen-0.1.0-beta Mod Extension:

Requirements:
BepInEx
YamIDotNet (YamIDotNet.dll)
VikingNPC (Norsemen.dll)

About:
- This is a mod-extension to Rusty's VikingNPC mod. This mod utilizes VikingNPC mod as a base. It adds some more features such as
  more jobs, NPC controls, container tags, and QoL such as persistence after NPC death and equipped items displayed correctly.
  This mod is based off of the Spring 2026 version of VikingNPC, after Viking raids & villages had been removed.
  I built this mod based of my love of Valheim but not wanting the world to feel so alone, to try building a mod for the first
  time, and to make the game something I'd enjoy even more. 
  I will make my extension available and may entertain suggestions for future features or bug updates. 
  I have received Rusty's blessing to continue updates to his base mod, which is needed for this mod to work.
  *Since I am making this for fun in my free time, testing has been limited as I am building this while I progress through the
   game at my own pace. If functionality suffers in later zones and you let me know, I'll take note but I can't promise quick
   updates. 

Jobs:
Lumberjack (Axe)
Miner (Pickaxe)
Fisherman (Fishing Rod & Bait)
Guard-Stationary (menu options)
Guard-Patrol (menu options)
Farmer (Cultivator)
Cook-Meats (meat-source Trophies)

Features:
Home-Point
Work-Point
Patrol-Point (Guards)
"Go To" (Home/Work) interaction commands
Tagged storage containers
Focused Work (Lumberjack, Miner, Fisher, & Cook)
Shifts (Day / Night)

Behaviors:
- Untamed NPC
	- They will appear randomly during your adventures.
	- You will need to feed them to "tame" them. They will not be afraid of you.
	- You can 'Steal' from them. -this might make them hostile towards you.
	- If they die while "wild", they cannot be resurrected.

- Once a NPC is tamed, the player will have the following interaction options:
	[E] Follow / Stay
	[Shift + E] Rename
	[LeftAlt + E] Open (NPC Inventory)

- When the NPC's Inventory is open, Button option should appear along the bottom:
	Aggressive / Passive
		Aggressive will engage threats. Passive will run from threats.
	Guard / Patrol
		Guard will make NPC stay in that spot/ Patrol will let the NPC move about.
	Public / Private
		Public allows other players to interact with NPC, Private allows on the host-player to interact with NPCs
	Assign Home
		Will record the spot the NPC is currently standing as it's Home-Point.
	Assign Work
		Will record the spot the NPC is currently standing as it's Work-Point.
	*Assign Patrol B
		For NPCs assigned as "Guards", Will record the spot the NPC is currently standing as it's Patrol Point.

- Once a Home and or Work Point has been assigned, the player will have the following interaction options:
	[H] Go Home  (NPC goes to Home-Point)
	[K] Go To Work (NPC goes to Work-Point)

- Shifts: Day / Night
	- Day Shifts occur when the "Day" number is displayed. NPCs without a torch in their inventory will 
	  proceed to their Work-Point. Once at their Work-Point, they will perform whatever job has been assigned.
	- Night Shifts occur at night (when the "Cold" status appears on the player outside while sun is down).
          NPCs with a torch in their inventory will proceed to their Work-Point. Once at their Work-Point, 
	  they will perform whatever job has been assigned.

- Eating:
	- NPCs with food in their inventory will periodically consume it to maintain a "Happy" status.
		> NPC must be in a "Happy" status for them to perform their assigned job.
        - NPCs consume food in their inventory when they are in a "Hungry" status.
		> NPC in a "Hungry" status might not perform their assigned job.
	- To tell the status of the NPC, mouse over the NPC. Next to their name will show their status.
		'Name ( Status )'
	- The player can feed an NPC by placing a food on their action bar and pressing the corresponding key
	  of the food while mousing over the NPC.
	- The NPC will not consume food or be fed while in a "Happy" status.

- Following:
	- NPC will follow the player if set.
	- NPC will teleport to player's position if the separation distance exceeds 20-30m & health is above 10%.
		- this includes portals & dungeons.
	- NPC will attempt to sit/hold on if following you on a boat. 

- Tamed NPC Death:
	- NPC will drop a tomb upon death.
	- Player can interact with the tomb:
		> [E] View the inventory
		> [Shift + E] Resurrect NPC
	- ***It is HIGHLY recommended you save after resurrecting an NPC. 
		- There is a chance that exiting the game without saving, after an NPC has be resurrected, may
		  result in the NPC completely disappearing (along with items in their inventory) upon logging
		  back in.

- Typical NPC Cycle:
	- NPC becomes active at start of their shift. Travel paths to Work-Points (or 'Cooked Foods' containers if established) are calculated.
	- NPC in travel will open doors within 4m of NPC. (unintended door opening may occur)
	- NPC will collect 3 cooked food items if "Cooked Foods" container is withing their shift-start radius.
	- NPC will reach their established Work-Points and begin their jobs.
	- Once the Shift change trigger is hit, NPCs with jobs that produce items will attempt to deposit items in tagged containers (if established)
	- NPC will travel to Home-Point (or closest spot to it if obstructed).
	- NPC in travel will open doors within 4m of NPC. These doors should close behind them IF that NPC opened it.

Tagged Storage Containers:
- EVERY kind of container will now show a button for assigning a tag to it.
- Current tags and function:
	- "Deposit: None" - NPCs will not interact with it.
	- "Deposit: Farmer" - NPC meeting 'Farmer' job criteria will place harvested items in this container.
	- "Deposit: Lumberjack" - NPC meeting 'Lumberjack' job criteria will place wood, sap, feathers in this container.
	- "Deposit: Miner" - NPC meeting 'Miner' job criteria will place ore & stone in this container.
	- "Deposit: Fisherman" - NPC meeting 'Fisherman' job criteria will place fish in this container.
	- "Deposit: Raw Meat" - NPC meeting 'Cook' job criteria will take raw meats from this container.
	- "Deposit: Cooked Foods" - NPC meeting 'Cook' job criteria will place cooked foods in this container.
				  - NPCs within a radius of this box will take 3 cooked foods from this container.

Jobs:
- Lumberjack: (Axe)
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must place an axe, to the appropriate strength of the tree to collect, in the NPC's inventory.
	* Player can focus the NPC to chop specific trees by placing the corresponding tree seed in their inventory.
	* Player should set the NPC's Work-Point near desired trees to collect.
	- NPC will work in the "Happy" state (fed).
	- NPC's work area is 50m-radius from Work-Point.
	- NPC will randomly run to a tree that meets their job criteria, and begin chopping.
		> The tree chopped provides the wood, sap, & feathers that would normally drop if felled.
	- The trees will NOT be damaged/felled. -no need for replanting.
	- At end of shift, if a storage container tagged "Lumberjack" is nearby, all Wood, Sap, Feather items 
	  in NPC's inventory will be deposited in that box.

- Miner: (Pickaxe)
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must place a pickaxe, to the appropriate strength of the ore to collect, in the NPC's inventory.
	* Player can focus the NPC to tap specific ore-nodes by placing the corresponding metal bar in their inventory.
	* Player should set the NPC's Work-Point near desired ore-node to collect.
	- NPC will work in the "Happy" state (fed).
	- NPC's work area is 50m-radius from Work-Point.
	- NPC will randomly run to a ore-node that meets their job criteria, and begin tapping.
		> The ore-node tapped provides the ore & stone.
	- The ore-node will NOT be damaged/destroyed. 
	- At end of shift, if a storage container tagged "Miner" is nearby, all ore & stone in NPC's inventory 
	  will be deposited in that box.

- Fisher: (Fishing Rod & Bait)
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must place a Fishing Rod & Bait in the NPC's inventory.
	* Player can focus the NPC to catch specific biome fish by placing the corresponding biome bait in their inventory.
	* Player should set the NPC's Work-Point near the coast & near desired fish.
	- NPC will work in the "Happy" state (fed).
	- NPC's work area is 50m-radius from Work-Point.
	- NPC will randomly run to a low point near water meets their job criteria, and begin casting.
	- Fish are NOT physically caught. 
	- At end of shift, if a storage container tagged "Fisherman" is nearby, all fish in NPC's inventory 
	  will be deposited in that box.

- Guard: 
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must select "Aggressive" & "Guard" from the NPC inventory buttons.
	* Player should equip NPC with appropriate weapons / gear. (Avoid Axes)
	* Player should set the NPC's Work-Point at where they want an NPC to defend.
	* Player can make the guard patrol by assigning a "Patrol B" point.
		> Have NPC follow the player to the spot the Guard should patrol to, then select "Assign Patrol B".
	- NPC will work in any state.
	- NPC will target threats in the area, roughly < 20m-radius from the NPC.
	- If assigned to patrol, NPC will walk between their established Work-Point & Patrol-Point.
	- NPCs on patrol will re-ignite any light source & close any open doors within 4m of the NPC.
	- Weapon durability can diminish & projectiles will be consumed upon use.


- Farmer: (Cultivator)
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must plant seed or food bearing plant (either will produce the food).
	* Player must build beehives near established Work-Point(for Honey).
	* Player must place a cultivator in the NPC's inventory.
	* Player sets the NPC's Work-Point in middle of planted field.
	- NPC will work in the "Happy" state (fed).
	- NPC's work area is 5m-radius from Work-Point.
	- NPC will randomly walk to a point and kneel down (as if picking).
		> If a plant is there, a chance to pick it and collect honey(if hive is near) is achieved.
	- Plant will NOT physically be picked. -no need for replanting.
	- At end of shift, if a storage container tagged "Farmer" is nearby, all plant foods in NPC's inventory 
	  will be deposited in that box. 

- Cook (Meats): 
	** It's recommended to not place other job related items in NPC's inventory.
	* Player must select "Passive" & "Guard" from the NPC inventory buttons.
	* Player must establish the following:
		- A cooking station
		- A container set to "Raw Meat"
		- *Recommended: A container set to "Cooked Food" within 20m of Work-Point.
	* Player must place the corresponding trophy of the meat's source in the NPC's inventory.
		- NPC will only cook "Raw Fish" without a trophy.
	* Player sets the NPC's Work-Point near the cooking station & "Raw Meat" container.
	- NPC will work in any state. (Note: NPC will eat the cooked food in it's inventory if in a "Hungry" status)
	- NPC will got to and check the "Raw Meat" container for meat to cook. 
		> If there are no meats matching the trophies or raw fish, the NPC will go back to Work-Point and check again in 2 mins.
	- NPC will put the meat from their inventory on to the cooking station.
	- NPC will collect ANY cooked meat and coal from cooking station.
	- Meats are removed from container (products are NOT simulated).
	- At end of shift, NPC will stay until all cooked meat is removed from cooking station.
	- At end of shift, if a storage container tagged "Cooked Food" is nearby, all cooked foods in NPC's inventory 
	  will be deposited in that box.
