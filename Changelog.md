# Changelog

All notable changes to LivingNorsemen will be documented here.

## 0.1.0-beta

Initial public beta release.

### Added

- Home and Work Point assignment for tamed Norsemen.
- Go Home and Go To Work commands.
- Day and Night work shifts.
- Profession-based tagged storage containers.
- Automated profession resource deposits.
- Cooked food distribution to nearby NPCs.
- Lumberjack profession with tool and resource targeting.
- Miner profession with tool and ore targeting.
- Fisherman profession with fishing rod, bait, and biome-focused fishing.
- Farmer profession with crop and beehive harvesting.
- Cook profession using Raw Meat and Cooked Foods storage.
- Trophy-controlled meat cooking.
- Raw fish cooking without a trophy.
- Support for Deer, Boar, Neck, Wolf, and Draugr-based cooking recipes.
- Stationary Guard behavior.
- Guard patrol routes using Work Point and Patrol B.
- Guard patrol door-closing and fireplace-relighting behavior.
- NPC door interaction while traveling between Home and Work Points.
- Profession-focused work behaviors designed around settlement automation.

### Beta Notes

- LivingNorsemen requires 'VikingNPC Continued'.
- Initial beta development and testing has primarily followed normal gameplay progression.
- Later-biome interactions and unusual profession/item combinations may require additional testing.
- Features and behaviors may change during beta development.
